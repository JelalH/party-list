Privacy Policy

Party List · last updated 8 November 2026

Party List is a small app for keeping a guest list for a party. This page explains what it stores and why.

What we collect

  • Your account: when you sign in with Google or Apple we receive your name and email address. We use them only to sign you in and keep your list linked to you.
  • Your guest list: the party details and guests you type in (names, emails, phone numbers, notes and entry codes).

Payments

Party List is free for up to 20 guests. Premium is a one-off £2.99 payment. On the website it is handled by Stripe; in the apps by Apple or Google. We never see your card details. We only store whether your account has Premium.

How it is stored

Your list is saved on your device and in our database (hosted by Supabase), so it appears on every device where you sign in. Access rules in the database mean only your account can read or change your list.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share your list with anyone. Nothing is sent to your guests unless you choose to send it yourself (for example with the "Text code" or "Share" buttons, which open your own apps).

Your guests' details

If you add other people's contact details, please only add what they're happy for you to keep for the party.

Deleting your data

You can delete guests at any time in the app. To delete your account and everything in it, ask using the contact link below and it will be removed.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on GitHub.

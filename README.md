# Party List

A minimal guest-list app: light/dark, your own order or A→Z / Z→A, +1s, short entry codes, PDF/Word export.
Sign in with Google or Apple to keep the list on your phone and computer (Supabase).

- `app/` – the app itself (static files, installable on phones)
- `supabase/schema.sql` – the database table and its access rules
